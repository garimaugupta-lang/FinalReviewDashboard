Project

Phoenix

TSA exit planning for FIS

Supported FIS in planning the IT transition for its Credit Issuing business and TSA exit, with focus on reducing dependency on GPN systems while protecting business continuity.

Key Contributions

Enabled CXO decision-making on Island Browser by framing 2 options across 6 controls and 4 risk areas
Surfaced TIS access gaps and grouped users by migration priority before laptop movement decisions
Cleaned and analyzed 35k+ Cisco assets across 87 addresses, while identifying 69% data gaps for stakeholder action
Framed 3 options for FIS executives to restart asset registration and unblock Q3 revenue enablement
Proposed a 12-step roadmap for the 6-week SC engagement, clarifying ownership, timelines, and handoffs
Created a 148-product inventory separating NA and International products and flagging 55 priorities to improve tracking clarity
Mapped 20+ Capital One applications to different agreements for transition ownership clarity
Converted 10+ workshop sessions into a centralized action and decision log for clearer ownership, status tracking, and follow-up

Impact

Restored FIS's path to Q3 revenue enablement - 3 executive options and a 6-week roadmap gave FIS a clear path

Enabled informed Leadership Decision on Island Browser controls, reducing productivity friction during EUC migration

35000+ cleaned asset records gave the team a reliable, connected data foundation for transition execution
