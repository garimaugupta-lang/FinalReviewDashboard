The first two weeks were all about stepping in - starting with induction, leader sessions, PwC values, networking, and an AI prototype challenge, followed by a 2-day bootcamp that introduced me to deal phases and set the stage for project work.

First Contributions & Project Ramp-Up

Excited and slightly nervous, I began contributing to two projects across different deal phases - Phoenix & Rioja . With every deliverable, my curiosity and the team’s mentorship made the learning curve both exciting and rewarding.

Mid-Review & CSR Impact

These two weeks brought together growth and giving back, from my mid-review with my DL and coach, which helped me reflect on feedback and focus areas, to a CSR planting activity that gave me a chance to connect with fellow interns while contributing to nature.

Final Sprint & Presentations

The final sprint brought everything together - project work, re-invest initiatives, AI ideas, and the final review.
